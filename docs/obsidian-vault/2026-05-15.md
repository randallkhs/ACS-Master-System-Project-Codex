## Architecture Understanding

- ACS FSM is a long-term Field Service Management platform, not a temporary script replacement.
- PostgreSQL should become the operational source of truth.
- External systems such as Google Calendar, Google Sheets, FastField, Verizon Connect, and AI providers must be integration adapters.
- Backend business logic belongs in services, validators, workflow engines, repositories, and domain modules.
- Frontend should consume APIs and structured backend responses; it should not contain operational business logic.

## Workflow Understanding

- First real module: Dispatch Operations Engine.
- Core flow: Calendar import -> normalization -> classification -> validation/confidence -> Manual Review -> routing -> Sheets/FastField transitional output -> audit logging.
- Current operational priority is next-day or next-business-day schedule processing; exact date logic still needs operational confirmation.
- Existing CleaningWorkflow concepts to preserve include preview-before-write/send, Water Emergency separation, route grouping, template copy reuse, and audit/debug output.

## Business Logic Understanding

- Standard jobs are usually single-day, single-visit work.
- Water Emergency jobs are multi-day, multi-visit operational workflows with equipment and technician history requirements.
- Cancellation detection must tolerate misspellings and route uncertainty to Manual Review.
- AM/PM and state markers affect routing and scheduling.
- Manual Review Queue is a core operational safety system.

## AI Rule Understanding

- AI may assist with classification, anomaly detection, confidence scoring, summaries, and recommendations.
- AI must not override operators, auto-dispatch uncertain jobs, auto-confirm cancellations, or close Water Emergency workflows.
- Deterministic validation and human review override AI suggestions.
